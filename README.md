Built in Unity 6 for the Meta Quest 3, this VR floral arrangement experience bridges the gap between spatial computing and botanical art.
It is designed for both hobbyists looking for a grounding creative outlet and aspiring florists who need a cost-effective,
zero-waste environment to practice arrangement techniques.
The concept originated from my own desire to open a flower shop. Faced with the high upfront costs of commercial refrigeration
and the environmental waste of practicing with perishable stems,
I turned to my background in VR development. By translating floral mechanics into virtual space, this project offers a sustainable,
accessible sandbox where technology and artistic expression meet.
